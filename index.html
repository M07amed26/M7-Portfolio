About Me

I'm Mohamed, a computer science student at the University of Algiers 1, training to become a software engineer and web designer. Every day, I'm balancing my university coursework with hands-on practice — building more web pages, learning new techniques, and pushing myself to design better.

My focus is on creating modern, clean, and intentional web pages. I care about how a site looks, but also how it feels to use. I want every interface I build to be intuitive, functional, and visually sharp. I'm not waiting until graduation to start — I'm training now, one project at a time. And at the center of everything I'm building is M7 — my big project. It's the work I want to be known for, and you'll see its name everywhere across my portfolio.

Outside of coding, I'm deeply interested in astronomy. There's something about looking at the night sky that reminds me to think big and pay attention to detail. I also stay active through football and regular gym training. For me, discipline in sports feeds discipline in design.

I believe the best work comes from a curious mind and a strong body. I'm building the skills today to become the software engineer and web designer I want to be tomorrow. M7 is the proof.
